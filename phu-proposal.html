Strategic Proposal & Rebuild Roadmap

Modern digital flagship and search engine for PHU.

Pfeiffer Hill Upholstery represents Australian manufacturing quality, over 40 years in the trade, and rugged durability across Southern Australia. This proposal sets out a modern Next.js 15 platform paired with a high-intent search strategy, designed to increase quote requests and project briefs coming directly to PHU.

Initial visual assets are already in place: a refined colour palette, logo package, and interactive HTML/CSS demo landing page. These show the direction of the design and the technical approach before any further investment.

Core Platform Architecture

Focused on speed, clarity and simple paths to a quote.

The proposed platform runs on Next.js 15 static architecture for fast, reliable page delivery and sub-second mobile navigation. Content is organised into five hubs:

Home — hero, trust metrics, visual service pillars and PHU sub-brands.
Services — grouped into Auto & Hot Rods, Marine & Houseboats, Tarps & Agriculture, and Custom Industrial.
About — Terry’s story, Mannum roots, and heavy-duty welded construction standards.
Our Work — visual gallery grid showing auto, marine, tarps and industrial projects.
Contact & Quote — instant three-step brief form and sticky mobile click-to-call.

Growth & SEO Engine

Optional expansion for long-term search and content.

Option 2 adds a search and content layer on top of the platform. Technical SEO and JSON-LD schema describe PHU as a LocalBusiness with specific services, feeding structured data into Google’s Local Pack and AI search experiences.

Five high-intent articles (800–1,000 words) target welded PVC tarps in the SA climate, marine upholstery care for ski boats on the Murray River, custom leather interiors for classic and European restoration work, HeaderTarps and grain harvester bin covers, and heavy-duty trailer canopy fabrics.

Three product and geo landing pages support header tarps, Riverland boat covers, and classic car upholstery searches, each with clear calls-to-action for quotes and photos.
