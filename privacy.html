Privacy Policy

1. Information Collection

We collect information that you provide directly to us through our contact form, including your name, email address, and phone number. This information is used solely to respond to your enquiries and provide quotes for our services.

2. Use of Enquiries and Quote Details

Details regarding your upholstery or tarp requirements are used to generate accurate quotes and manage your project. We do not share these details with third parties except as necessary to fulfill your request.

We use cookies to understand how visitors interact with our website. This helps us improve the user experience. You can choose to decline non-essential cookies through our cookie banner.

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information we hold from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access. However, no data transmission over the internet can be guaranteed as completely secure.
